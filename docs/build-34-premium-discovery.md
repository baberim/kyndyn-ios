# Build 34 — Premium discovery

Build 34 makes Kyndyn Premium visible at the point where an authenticated
parent is already making family decisions.

## Placement

- Free households see a prominent trial card near the top of the protected
  Parent dashboard.
- Free parents also see a compact upgrade card on Home so Premium is
  discoverable during normal use. It is hidden from child profiles and from
  every Home screen once Premium is active.
- Onboarding explains that everyday quests, progress, and sharing are free and
  that Premium adds optional planning, personalization, insights, and
  Apple-device extras.
- The card does not appear on child-facing Home, Quests, Settings, or Profiles.
- Once Premium is active, the promotional card becomes a quiet membership row
  for plan management and purchase restoration.
- Premium is no longer buried under Device and privacy.

## Customer language

The purchase screen describes outcomes rather than internal product concepts:
planning ahead, personalization, progress visibility, and Apple-device
features. It does not refer to a “family loop,” entitlements, ownership types,
or other implementation language.

This follows the useful pattern employed by successful freemium products:
short benefit-led headlines, concrete feature descriptions, a clear trial, and
reassurance that the useful core product remains available.

## Current boundary

Build 34 changes discovery and presentation only. It does not put an existing
Kyndyn feature behind Premium. StoreKit remains the source of purchase truth,
and subscription expiration never removes family data or earned progress.
